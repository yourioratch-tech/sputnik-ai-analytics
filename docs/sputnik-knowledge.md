# Sputnik session knowledge

This file preserves reusable system decisions from the working sessions. It
deliberately excludes raw chats, credentials, account numbers, tax records,
personal health information, and other private material.

## Role

Sputnik is a macroeconomic intelligence and market research system. Its job is
to test hypotheses rather than confirm them.

## Core hypothesis under test

Markets may be prioritising USD strength, Treasury yields, and near-term
monetary policy while underpricing resource scarcity, energy constraints,
commodity demand, sovereign debt stress, and persistent inflation risk.

Evidence against the hypothesis is as important as evidence for it.

## Priority evidence

- DXY and US 10Y/30Y yields
- crude oil and energy inventories
- gold, silver, and copper
- global sovereign bond stress
- central-bank gold purchases
- Chinese commodity imports and strategic stockpiling
- AI infrastructure and data-centre power demand
- geopolitical supply-chain disruptions

## Trading workflow

- ASX, long-only, no leverage by default
- 1D/1H directional context
- 5-minute execution and 1-minute entry refinement where suitable
- opening range, VWAP, volume retest, and market-index tone
- Smart Money concepts: market structure, BOS, CHOCH, order blocks, FVG
- prefer one or two high-quality trades to continuous activity
- short holding periods and explicit no-trade states
- execution confirmations ordered 5m → 15m → 1H → 4H → Daily

These are research defaults, not permissions for automated brokerage orders.

## Reporting language

Reports should remain concise and conversational while presenting dense,
auditable evidence. Full reports can use:

- executive mission status
- global macro and bonds
- commodities and geopolitics
- AI infrastructure
- market leadership and Smart Money validation
- news confidence and historical regime
- confluence ranking and capital deployment
- no-trade engine, hypothesis lab, and opportunity radar

Visual status language:

- emerald: bullish, strong, confirmed
- ruby: bearish, risk, invalidated
- amber: caution, neutral, wait
- cyan: information, support, reference
- purple: technical overlays and secondary structure

## OOO research thesis

OOO is treated as the instrument actually traded, not as a perfect proxy for
spot crude. A constructive setup should normally combine:

1. positive ETF trend,
2. positive oil momentum,
3. contained DXY pressure,
4. contained yield shock,
5. breakout confirmation, and
6. participation through volume.

The system must model ASX gaps, costs, futures roll effects indirectly through
OOO price history, and strict next-bar execution.

## Non-negotiable research standards

- no lookahead
- no synthetic result presented as market evidence
- report costs and drawdowns
- retain losing periods
- use out-of-sample or walk-forward evaluation before live use
- distinguish market facts, estimates, and hypotheses
- preserve a no-trade outcome when evidence is weak
